Sukna Ventures

The name you heard was Sukna, a Riyadh venture firm. “Sukuk” is a different word for an Islamic finance instrument. This page keeps the facts deliberately Soft unless marked Proven.

Soft What Sukna is

Riyadh early-stage VC

Sukna Ventures is an early-stage venture capital firm in Riyadh, with a digital transformation focus. In plain English, it backs young companies and helps build a venture portfolio.

Why it matters: put Sukna in the venture-fund bucket, not the sukuk or fixed-income bucket. The public team page is useful orientation, not proof of every investment activity or mandate.

Word bank

GP General Partner · runs the fund, chooses investments and manages the portfolio.

LP Limited Partner · commits capital to a fund but does not run its daily investments.

FoF Fund of funds · a vehicle that invests in other funds rather than mainly investing directly in startups.

VC Early-stage venture capital · investment in young, high-growth companies, usually before they are mature public businesses.
