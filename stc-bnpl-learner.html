STC vs BNPL

Two labels people mix up. One is the rulebook name. One is the product shape.

STC Short-Term Credit

The UAE regulatory bucket for short-dated consumer credit (often instalments). Think: “what licence and rules apply.”

Why it matters: if someone says they do BNPL in the UAE, ask which licence / STC rules they sit under.

BNPL Buy Now Pay Later

The customer experience: buy today, pay in splits. The product marketing name — not itself a full legal category.

Why it matters: BNPL can look “fintech cute” while the real risk is still credit risk + fee rules + hardship handling.

One sentence to remember

BNPL is often how it’s sold; STC is often how it’s regulated.

Your SME question (leading vs lagging)

Lagging = already broken. Leading = getting weaker.

  • Lagging: missed payments, collections, worse CIA/Etihad file, bounced cheques, covenant breaches.
  • Leading: slower invoice collection, rising overdue suppliers, thinner cash buffer, owner drawing more, customer concentration, margin squeeze, “extend me 30 days” becoming normal.
Dinner line: “What do you watch before the CIA file turns red?”
