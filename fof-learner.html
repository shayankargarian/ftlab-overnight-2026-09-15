When funds invest in funds

You heard fund managers talk about money going into other funds, not only into startups. That lane has a name.

FoF Fund of funds

A fund whose job is mostly to invest in other venture funds (or a mix of funds), not to write every cheque to startups itself.

Why it matters: you meet people who control capital without being the day-to-day startup investor.

LP vs GP

LP = Limited Partner — puts money into a fund (pension, family office, FoF, endowment).

GP = General Partner — runs the fund, picks deals, earns fees + carry.

Dinner table: fund managers are usually GPs. People who “invest in funds” are often LPs or FoF GPs.

Soft vs Proven (again)

Soft dinner story / Carta chart — good for learning.

Proven primary doc or your own extract — good for decisions.
