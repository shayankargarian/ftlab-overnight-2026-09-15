Word bank
SAFE

Simple Agreement for Future Equity. Cash in now; equity later at a priced round. Usually not classic debt.

Convertible note

Similar economic idea, but usually debt-like (interest, maturity) until it converts into equity.

Valuation cap

Ceiling on the company valuation used when the SAFE/note converts. Protects the early investor if the next round prices high.

Pre-seed

On Carta Soft: any fundraising on convertibles before the company's first priced equity round.

Instrument

One SAFE or note contract. A single "deal" can stack many instruments (Carta Soft: big deals often 10+).

Caps & concentration

Record average · thin top end

Average instrument size hit a Soft record $276k (+27% YoY). Few pre-seed deals exceed $2.5M; those that do usually stack 10+ instruments. At the 90th percentile, valuation caps on SAFEs larger than $2.5M can reach $100M (AI-linked Soft).

5 takeaways for CoS
  1. Concentration: ~same cash, far fewer instruments; avg cheque at a Soft record $276k.
  2. Ask the right question: dollars vs deal count before saying the market is "up" or "down".
  3. AI share Soft: ~49% of pre-seed dollars H1 2026; high, perhaps plateauing vs 2025's ~50%.
  4. Paper literacy: SAFE / note / valuation cap / instrument are the dinner vocabulary.
  5. Scope: US Carta Soft sample. Learning colour for New Genre / GCC walks, not MENA proof.
