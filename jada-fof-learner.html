Jada Fund of Funds

What Jada actually does as a PIF fund of funds that LPs into other managers. Learning only. Soft dinner bridge stays Soft; Proven only where tied to Jada’s public site (freeze 2026-09-16).

Word bank

FoF Fund of funds · a vehicle that invests mainly in other funds, not mainly by writing every cheque to startups itself.

LP Limited Partner · commits capital into a fund; does not run day-to-day deal picking for that fund.

GP General Partner · runs the fund, selects investments, earns fees and carry.

PIF FoF Jada’s public role · PIF-owned FoF that allocates as an LP across VC, PE, and private debt managers.

Why it matters: dinner talk about “investing in funds” is often this LP / FoF lane, not the startup-cheque lane.

Proven What Jada says it is

PIF-owned fund of funds (2018)

Jada was launched by the Public Investment Fund in 2018 (Council of Ministers resolution). FAQs state it is a privately held company, wholly owned by PIF, with investment capital coming directly from PIF.

Why it matters: put Jada in the sovereign FoF / LP bucket, not in the “another VC writing startup cheques” bucket.

Proven: About Us · FAQs · accessed 2026-09-16.

Proven What Jada invests in

VC, private equity, and private debt funds

  • Jada invests in venture capital, private equity, and private debt funds (Saudi-focused), which can in turn finance SME growth.
  • Mission (About Us): provide funding to Saudi SMEs through commercially sustainable investments in those three fund types.
  • FAQs: Jada does not directly invest in any startups; underlying funds allocate to companies.
Why it matters: the teachable pattern is FoF as LP into GPs across three private-market sleeves, not direct venture picking.

Proven: jada.com.sa · About · FAQs · accessed 2026-09-16.
